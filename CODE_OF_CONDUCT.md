# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces. Discuss technical decisions on their merits, assume good intent, and make room for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
